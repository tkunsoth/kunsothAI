Case study

The human–AI agent partnership that booked 48 demos in 3 months

The starting point

Our client's target customers are local businesses. They had early signs of fit across several markets:

Private clubsIndependent restaurants & barsLuxury salons & spasSpecialty cafés

Every one of those customers had found them through word of mouth.

The client lacked a proactive outreach system, which made their growth depend on referrals. This hindered the speed required to quickly test out different markets and scale with confidence.

The goal

They wanted to validate those markets and expand into similar ones, proactively. We designed an email program to book meetings every week, then used the live feedback from each week's conversations to sharpen the next week's targeting.

How we solved it

We took a human+agent approach. Outreach, client relations and reading market feedback were still driven by human judgement whereas, finding the right prospects, verifying the owners, sourcing accurate contact, scheduling emails was all agentic.

Over the course of 3 months, we reached 1,699 unique businesses in 8 markets, averaging about 4 meetings per week.

The sheer scale of this level of iteration was possible due to the time and effort saved by delegating playbook-driven work to AI.
